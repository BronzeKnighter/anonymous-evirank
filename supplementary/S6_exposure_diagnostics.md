# S6. Deployment-Oriented Exposure Diagnostics

These slices are descriptive auditing signals, not demographic or causal
fairness evidence. Reviewer-history volume is based on retrieved publication
records, career stage uses first-publication year as a proxy, and topic
popularity uses query-topic-frequency terciles.

EviRank improves acceptable hit@3 across all topic buckets and reduces
hard-negative top-1 incidence in the mid and head buckets. However, exposure of
high-volume reviewers rises slightly and the early-career proxy share falls.
Ranking quality and exposure fairness therefore cannot be treated as
interchangeable.

Exact slice statistics are in
[`exposure_diagnostics.csv`](exposure_diagnostics.csv).
